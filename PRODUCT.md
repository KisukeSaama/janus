# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Platform and security engineers administer Janus; application teams consume approved external APIs through it. Administrators work in a focused operational console where configuration accuracy and auditability matter more than novelty.

## Product Purpose

Janus keeps third-party credentials out of client applications while enforcing environment, provider, route, and method boundaries at a controlled proxy. Success means every outbound request is attributable, authorized, and incapable of revealing the underlying credential.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-made control plane: restrained under normal operation and unambiguous when risk or failure needs attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid “hacker terminal” theatrics, generic purple SaaS dashboards, ornamental security imagery, excessive cards, and dense enterprise screens that hide the active security boundary.

## Design Principles

1. Make the active environment impossible to miss.
2. Show authorization relationships in the language operators use.
3. Reveal metadata and status, never secret material.
4. Prefer familiar controls and explicit outcomes over clever interaction.
5. Keep high-risk actions deliberate and auditable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, full keyboard operation, visible focus, non-color status cues, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
